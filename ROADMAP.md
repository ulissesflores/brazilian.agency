# Roadmap

## This version

Portal with one dossier (001, Raio-X do Brasil por governo), the emblem, search metadata, sitemap, 404, seal and tests; GitHub Pages on `brazilian.agency`.

## Next

* **Briefings**: one-page fact checks against primary sources, numbered by year (the first: "Brasil volta a ser devedor externo" against the Central Bank's own definition).
* **Monitors**: series that refresh from official APIs with a growing hash chain (public debt, interest, paid amendments, deforestation).
* **English version** of the portal; the dossiers' technical documentation already exists in five languages.
* **In-browser seal check**: a page that recomputes the dossier's hash from the served bytes.
* **Redirects**: `brazillian.agency` (misspelling) and `coruja.cc` (short links) to this portal.

## Not planned

Any score of government merit; any content without a public source; any affiliation claim.
